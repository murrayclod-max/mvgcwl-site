New to the league?

The form puts your name on the Draft Board. It does not guarantee you a spot. New players who are not drafted do not play this year.

How the draft works

The 2027 league is 12 teams of 10 players. The committee places every player in one of 10 tiers. Each captain plays for their own team, in their own tier. The captains draft everyone else, returning players included, on Saturday, Dec 5.

GHIN numbers

If you have a GHIN number, enter it on the form. If you do not have one, we highly recommend getting one. You can still sign up without it. The form will ask a few questions about your game so the committee can place you in the right tier.
